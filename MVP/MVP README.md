## Game Enhancement Details

This refined version streamlines the gameplay progression to maintain high player engagement and improve pacing:

* **Streamlined Level Progression:** The total length has been consolidated from 7 levels (6 standard levels + 1 bonus round) down to **4 focused levels**.
* **Stage Restructuring:** Levels 1 through 3 now feature **2 stages each** while level 4 now feature a new gameplay mechanic with 3 stages each. 
* **Escalating Challenge:** As players progress, the number of distinct target gestures required reduces from **4 down to 1**, requiring players to consistently repeat and master the precise gesture target.
* **Cooperative Final Stage:** The final level transitions players to a new environment designed to work together. Players must join their hands to form a single shadow, working cooperatively as a team to complete the game.

> **Note:** Players must show an open palm to initialize and start the game.


# Game Rules & Objective

The **main objective** of the game is to successfully complete all 4 levels to upgrade the weapon before running out of lives and time
![alt text](<Station Pictures/IMG_2967.JPG>)
* **Beat the Clock:** Every stage is bound by a strict countdown timer. If the timer hits zero before you match the required gestures / shadows, you fail the stage and will have to restart

![alt text](<Station Pictures/Flow Of Game.png>)
* **Progressive Co-Op:** As you advance through the game, you realise that eventually both players must follow the same target gestures shown on the screen
![alt text](<Station Pictures/IMG_2967-1.JPG>)
* **The 3-Life Rule:** You begin your journey with 3 lives. If you fail a stage, you lose a life and are sent back to the start of the stage. Losing all lives results in Game Over

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/260da920-a780-43b9-9eba-ad06342f5895
* **The Ultimate Test:** Completing Level 3 unlocks the shadow charging round. Both player have to use 1 of their hands to form a shadow and must survive 3 of such rounds in order to finish the game

## Penalties
* **Running Out of Time:** Each stage has a countdown timer. Failing to hold the required gestures before the timer hits zero results in a failed charge

* **Losing Lives:** You begin with 3 lives. A time-out costs you a life, triggers a failure lighting state and sound effect, and triggers a rollback penalty. Losing all lives will result in starting over the whole game
